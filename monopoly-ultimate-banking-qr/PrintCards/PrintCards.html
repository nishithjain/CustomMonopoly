Card Printer

Card size: 86 × 54 mm

A4 layout: 2 columns × 5 rows = 10 cards per sheet

JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP and other browser-supported image formats are supported.

Pairing Logic: Files are paired by their base name.

Examples:
• E01_Boom_Town_Back_QR.png pairs with Boom_Town_Front.png
• E02_Crime_Down_Back_QR.png pairs with Crime_Down_Front.png

The system extracts the actual name (Boom_Town, Crime_Down, etc.) from both front and back files.

Note: Card identifiers are only displayed on the BACK of the card.
