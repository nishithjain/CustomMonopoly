# User Guide — Banking QR V1

Practical guide for tabletop Ultimate Banking with the Android app.

## Installing the app

1. Install the APK on your Android phone (API 26+).
2. Open **Banking QR** and allow **Camera** when asked.
3. No internet is required for normal play.

## Starting a game

1. Tap **NEW GAME**.
2. Scan each participating **Player card** (2–4 players).
3. Confirm each player shows **M1500**.
4. Tap **START GAME**.

## During play — scan a card

1. On the game screen, tap **SCAN CARD**.
2. Point the camera at a Property, Event, or Player QR.
3. Follow on-screen prompts (buy, rent payer, event targets, etc.).

## Buying property

1. Scan an **unowned Property**.
2. Tap **BUY**.
3. Scan the **landing player's** card.
4. Confirm purchase result and give the physical Property card to that player.

## Paying rent

1. Scan the **owned Property**.
2. Scan the **visitor's Player** card.
3. Confirm rent paid and updated rent level.

## Events

1. Scan an **Event** card.
2. Follow prompts (players, properties, confirm).
3. Complete any **physical instructions** shown (moves, card swaps, etc.).

## Bank actions

Tap **BANK ACTIONS** on the game screen:

| Action | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| **COLLECT GO** | Player passed or landed on GO during normal movement |
| **LOCATION** | Player pays M100 to move to a chosen property (no GO) |
| **JAIL** | Manage jailed player (fee, doubles release) |
| **UNDO LAST ACTION** | Cancel the most recent eligible banking transaction |
| **GAME STATUS** | Balances, jail, properties, active effects |
| **RECENT BANKING** | Transaction history |

## Auction

1. On unowned property, tap **AUCTION** (or from an Event choice).
2. Scan the landing player, then bid with **Player card scans** (+M20 each).
3. Wait for timer or complete bidding; winner pays and receives the property.

## Debt

If a payment cannot be covered by cash, the **Debt** screen opens automatically.

1. Select or scan a property to transfer (at **purchase price** value).
2. Complete settlement; follow physical card transfer instructions.

## Bankruptcy and winner

If a player cannot cover debt with cash + property values, the game ends. View the **winner** screen, then **RETURN HOME** or **NEW GAME**.

## Resume

- **RESUME GAME** — continue an active saved game.
- **RESUME SETUP** — finish player registration if setup was interrupted.

## Undo

- Only the **last eligible** banking action can be undone.
- **Events cannot be undone.**
- Undo is blocked during debt resolution.

## Troubleshooting QR scans

- Hold the card **flat**, fill most of the frame, avoid glare.
- Use good indoor lighting; try slight tilt if needed.
- Wrong card type → message shown; tap **SCAN CARD** again.
- Duplicate protection: holding a card still only registers **one** scan; use **SCAN ANOTHER** / new scan flow to scan again intentionally.

## What the app does not do

- Track token positions on the board
- Roll dice
- Replace physical Property/Event cards
- Allow player trading
