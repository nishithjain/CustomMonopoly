Card Printer

Images are loaded in lexicographic filename order.

Landscape images print at 86 × 54 mm: 2 columns × 5 rows = 10 cards per A4 sheet.

Portrait images print at 54 × 86 mm: 3 columns × 3 rows = 9 cards per A4 sheet.

JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP and other browser-supported image formats are supported.

The browser reads each image's pixel dimensions first and chooses the matching physical card orientation automatically.

Select Change orientation to rotate every image 90° and switch it to the opposite print layout.

If portrait and landscape images are mixed, their filename order is preserved and a new sheet starts when the orientation changes.
